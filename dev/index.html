Ways of working

Where we came from

  1. 2018

    Founded as a workstream of the ASA Biopharmaceutical Section's Safety Scientific Working Group with DIA. The paper

  2. 2019

    The Hepatic Safety Explorer and its clinical workflow, tested by more than 20 people from 10 organisations. safetyGraphics on CRAN

  3. 2020

    safetyGraphics version 2 covers all safety domains. R/Pharma slides

  4. 2026

    After a hiatus, the charts are rebuilt with AI agents as safety.viz and gsm.safety. Keynote recap

  5. 2027

    The group relaunches. The earlier site is kept as an archive.
