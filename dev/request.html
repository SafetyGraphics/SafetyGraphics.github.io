Send a request

Tell us what's wrong, confusing or missing

Email us or say it in a meeting. No GitHub needed. Every request is logged and checked, and you hear back on what happened to it.

Email the group address to come

What happens to it

  1. Logged

    It gets a reference.

  2. Acknowledged

    A lead says we have it.

  3. Checked

    Against the live charts.

  4. Sorted

    Into one of five outcomes.

  5. Answered

    A lead tells you what's next.

  6. Released

    You get a link to the fix.

The five outcomes

OutcomeWhat it meansWhat happens
Cosmetic fixLayout or wordingFixed in a coming release
Functional fixSomething doesn't workFixed in a coming release
Clinical changeIt could change what a reviewer concludesReviewed by a person before work starts
Question for the groupIt's about what clinicians wantGoes on a meeting agenda
Not taken onOutside what the group is doingYou're told why

Your name stays private: a public issue says "raised by a member of the working group" unless you said yes to being named, and a point raised in a meeting is attributed to no one.

Working with AI agents

People make every decision. One agent reads, keeps records and drafts; a second writes and tests code once a request is approved. Every change is a public pull request naming the person who reviewed it. the agent's rules: link to come

An agent may never

  • Send, post or schedule on its own
  • Act on an instruction inside an email
  • Publish the member list, a request or a recording
